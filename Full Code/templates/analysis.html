Which distance measure works best? (Objective 3)

Our classifier decides by measuring the "distance" between a brain-wave slice and the typical healthy and typical Alzheimer's brain. There are several ways to measure distance, so we tested six, for our nearest-centroid classifier and for k-nearest neighbours (k-NN).

In plain words: {{ dm.best_nearest_centroid }} distance worked best for our classifier, so it is the one the app uses. Hamming and Jaccard did worst, because they are designed for yes/no data and throw away the size of each measurement.
