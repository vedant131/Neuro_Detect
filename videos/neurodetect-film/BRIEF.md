# NeuroDetect — 3-minute cinematic project film

Capstone Part E video for Vedant Shinde & Rhythm Tyagi, MIT World Peace University, Pune (guide: Dr. Anagha Deshpande).

Message: a short resting EEG and five sensors can give an explainable, lightweight Alzheimer's screening signal — a research prototype, not a diagnosis.

Source of truth: `../../Downloads/Black book_NeuroDetect_Final_Edited.docx` and `Full Code/models/metrics.json`.

Scene plan and narration: `SPEC.md`, `SCRIPT.md`. The person's own scene list (10 scenes, fixed timings) is followed; no alternative storylines were pitched because the brief specified the film.

Accepted decisions:
- Voice and music via HeyGen (the person signs in).
- Scene 2 person: stylised, clearly illustrative silhouette — no photo of a real person.
- Product scene uses real screenshots of the running NeuroDetect app.
